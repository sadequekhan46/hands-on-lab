Formula 1

Twenty-two cars, eleven teams and the fastest circuit racing on the planet. Here’s how the sport works, in five minutes.

A race weekend

Every Grand Prix runs over three days. The result on Sunday sets the points, but the weekend is won earlier than most people think.

Friday

Practice

Teams test setups and tyres. Nothing here scores points.

Saturday

Qualifying

Drivers set their fastest lap to decide the starting grid.

Sunday

The race

Roughly 300 km, with at least one pit stop and a lot of strategy.

Points

The top ten finishers score points in each race. Drivers and teams each have their own championship.
